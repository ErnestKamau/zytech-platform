# The Golden Ratio

For centuries architects have used mathematical proportions.

One famous ratio is approximately

1 : 1.618

You don't need to force this everywhere.

But understanding proportional relationships helps create harmony.

The lesson isn't "always use the golden ratio."

The lesson is "avoid arbitrary numbers."

# White Space Is Structural

In architecture,

a room is defined by the empty volume inside it.

In typography,

meaning is often created by the empty space around the words.

This is why premium brands never cram text together.

---

Dense paragraphs

↓

↓

Smooth

↓

# Optical Alignment

Mathematics isn't enough.

Humans don't perceive perfect geometry perfectly.

Professional designers adjust by eye.

Examples

Icons may appear slightly above the mathematical center.

Large headings may require tiny spacing adjustments.

Buttons may appear more balanced with optical, rather than geometric, alignment.

Trust your eyes—but train them first.
