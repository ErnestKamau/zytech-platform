# Objective

The purpose of this module is to develop your visual observation skills.

Before creating beautiful interfaces, you must first learn to analyze them.

Professional designers don't copy.

They deconstruct.

---

# What is Visual Literacy?

Visual literacy is the ability to understand why an interface looks and feels the way it does.

Instead of saying

"This website looks good."

You begin saying

"This website feels premium because it uses:

• restrained typography
• generous whitespace
• layered surfaces
• consistent spacing
• subtle motion
• limited colors
• strong hierarchy"

That is visual literacy.

# Learn to Ignore Content

One of the biggest tricks designers use.

Ignore what the page says.

Study how it communicates.

Instead of reading

"Build Apps Faster"

Ask

Why is this headline huge?

Why is it centered?

Why is it white?

Why is there empty space around it?

Why is the paragraph narrow?

Ignore words.

Observe structure.

# Layer 1

Canvas

Dark background.

Large empty areas.

Nothing feels crowded.

---

# Layer 2

Typography

Very large heading.

Tiny supporting paragraph.

Strong contrast.

Minimal text.

Easy scanning.

---

# Layer 3

Spacing

Huge vertical gaps.

Wide horizontal breathing room.

Buttons separated.

Navigation relaxed.

Nothing touches.

---

# Layer 4

Components

Only two button styles.

One primary.

One secondary.

Very few borders.

Rounded corners.

Consistent radii.

---

# Layer 5

Colors

Mostly dark.

White typography.

Single accent color.

Very restrained.

Nothing competes.

---

# Layer 6

Motion

Almost invisible.

Hover feels natural.

Animations support content.

Nothing distracts.

---

# Result

The page feels premium.

Not because of gradients.

Not because of animations.

Because everything is intentional.
