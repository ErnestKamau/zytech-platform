# Customer Portal Workflow & UX

## Purpose

The portal is the customer's operational workspace.

## Navigation

```text
Overview
Orders
RFQs
Quotes
Invoices
Payments
Deliveries
Documents
Projects
Addresses
Company Profile
```

## Overview

Surface:

- Quotes awaiting review
- Orders in progress
- Unpaid invoices
- Upcoming deliveries
- Recent documents
- Recent activity

## Orders

View order details, status, items, invoices, deliveries, and documents.

## RFQs

Create RFQs, view submitted RFQs, respond to clarification, view status, and view related quotes.

## Quotes

Review pricing, download PDF, accept, request revision, reject, comment, and upload PO.

## Invoices

View/download invoices, payment status, pay where supported, and view outstanding balance.

## Deliveries

View delivery status, ETA, tracking information where available, and proof of delivery.

## Documents

Organize documents by business context:

- Quotes
- POs
- Invoices
- Delivery documents
- Technical documents
- Certificates
- Product documents

## UX Principle

Make the next valid action obvious.

```text
Quote awaiting review
[Review Quote]
```
